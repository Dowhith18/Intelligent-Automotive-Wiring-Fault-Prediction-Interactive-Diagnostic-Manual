Engine RPM Over Time

Analysis: Engine RPM shows the rotational speed of the engine's crankshaft. Maintaining RPM in the optimal zone (1500-3000 RPM) improves fuel efficiency and reduces engine wear.

  • Green Zone (Idle): 0-1500 RPM - Vehicle at rest or low load
  • Yellow Zone (Optimal): 1500-3000 RPM - Best fuel efficiency
  • Red Zone (High): Above 3000 RPM - Higher fuel consumption and wear

Vehicle Speed Analysis

Analysis: Speed analysis helps identify driving patterns and efficiency. Maintaining consistent speeds in the optimal range (40-88 km/h) maximizes fuel economy.

  • Low Speed: 0-40 km/h - City driving, traffic
  • Optimal: 40-88 km/h - Best fuel efficiency range
  • High Speed: 88-120 km/h - Highway cruising
  • Very High: Above 120 km/h - Increased fuel consumption

Vehicle Acceleration Behavior

Analysis: Acceleration patterns reveal driving behavior. Smooth acceleration and braking improve fuel efficiency and reduce wear on brake components.

  • Green Points: Smooth acceleration/deceleration - Efficient driving
  • Orange Points: Hard acceleration (>0.3g) - Increased fuel consumption
  • Red Points: Hard braking (<-0.2g) - Inefficient, increased wear
Tip: Anticipate traffic flow and maintain steady speeds to minimize harsh acceleration and braking events.
