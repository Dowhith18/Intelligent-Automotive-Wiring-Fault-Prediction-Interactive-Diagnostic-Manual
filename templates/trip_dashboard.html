Engine RPM

How efficiently you used your rev range

Engine RPM (revolutions per minute) reveals how hard the engine is working. Staying between 1,000-3,000 RPM keeps the engine in its efficient operating range. Extended time above 5,000 RPM increases heat, fuel consumption, and wear.

  • > 5,000 RPM: High rpm means higher fuel usage.
  • 1,000 - 3,000 RPM: Ideal range for most cruising scenarios.
  • < 1,000 RPM: Extended idling wastes fuel without covering distance.

Vehicle Speed

Speed profile throughout the trip

Higher speeds push more air out of the way and need more engine power. The highlighted green zone marks efficient cruising speeds (roughly 50–100 km/h). Staying in this band keeps fuel usage balanced and reduces driver fatigue.

  • > 100 km/h: Aerodynamic drag grows quickly and efficiency drops.
  • 50 - 100 km/h: Ideal expressway speeds for most vehicles.
  • < 50 km/h: Frequent stops or slow traffic reduce efficiency.

Driving Dynamics

Acceleration and braking behavior

Rapid changes in acceleration and harsh braking events can reduce fuel efficiency and increase wear. Smooth inputs keep the drivetrain in balance and offer passengers a comfortable ride.

  • Rapid acceleration: Burns more fuel to reach target speed.
  • Coasting: Taking your foot off the accelerator saves fuel.
  • Hard braking: Converts momentum to heat, wasting energy.
